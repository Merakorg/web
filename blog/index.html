Shutting down operations

Hi all, During the last year, the cost of operation has long surpassed the rewards we were receiving from the networks we were validating on. As a result, we have made the difficult decision to cease operations on all networks. Thank for all your support and trust in us. We have enjoyed being part of these communities and wish you all the best in the future.
